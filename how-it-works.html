ScholarDone is the easiest way to outsource your academic heavy lifting. Here is how we ensure quality and integrity.

01

Post Your Requirements

Start by filling out our smart task form. Upload your rubric, syllabus, and any specific instructions from your professor. The more detail you provide, the better the final result.

  • Select subject & task type
  • Set a firm deadline
  • Define your budget
02

Expert Matching

Our system identifies the top 3 experts in our network who specialize in your specific topic. We look at their past performance, subject expertise, and current availability.

Once matched, you'll receive a notification. You can message your expert to clarify details or check on progress at any time.

03

Quality Assurance

Before the work reaches you, it goes through a secondary review. We check for plagiarism, adherence to instructions, and formatting accuracy (APA, MLA, etc.).

If something isn't right, the expert must fix it before the task is marked as "Under Review" for you.

04

Final Approval

Review the final document in your dashboard. If you need tweaks, just click "Request Revision" — it's free and unlimited. Once you're happy, approve the task to release funds and download your file.
